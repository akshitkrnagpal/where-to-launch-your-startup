## How to use this list

1. Start with free platforms that match your product category.
2. Prepare one launch kit with a short description, screenshots, logo, founder profile, and tracking links.
3. Schedule launches across several days instead of publishing everywhere at once.
4. Pay for priority only when the audience, backlink, or guaranteed date supports your launch goal.
5. Track referral traffic, signups, backlinks, and conversions from every platform.
